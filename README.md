## About
«Поділля» is a multi-purpose complex in Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine, located at vul. Lesi Ukrainky, 41B. It combines a hotel with a gym, a sauna, and consultations from five specialists: a urologist, an ENT doctor, an ophthalmologist, a gynecologist, and a surgeon. The page positions the complex around three themes: accommodation, activity, and health, and directs visitors to call for details on rooms, pricing, and schedules, which are not published on the site.
